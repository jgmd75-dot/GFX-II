Amps & drive

Preamp

A colour preamp with four voicings, a three-band EQ boost and a VU meter.

Overview

Preamp is a stage you put in front of anything that needs warmth, weight or a bit of dirt. Choose one of four voicings, set how hard to drive it, and shape the top end with TONE.

CLEAN adds no drive at all - use it with the EQ BOOST as a clean boost. VIBE recreates the preamp of a classic 60s pedal: extra low-mid weight, a soft, lopsided saturation and a rounded top that gets darker as you drive it. ZEN is a smooth, symmetrical, amp-like breakup. TRANS is a single-transistor drive: lopsided, it bites earlier and turns furry when pushed.

In every voicing except CLEAN a gentle, program-dependent compression (sag) sits in front of the drive, and it grows as you turn DRIVE up, like a real preamp running out of headroom. The EQ BOOST in front adds three gentle peaks a decade apart to push the source into the drive.

Reach for it for

  • Warming up DI guitar, bass and keys
  • Driving synths and drum loops into gentle grit
  • A clean, broad EQ lift on a bus (CLEAN with BOOST)
  • Fuzzy, transistor-style edge on a lead
  • A little analogue movement with DRIFT

Controls

VOICE

CLEAN
No drive and no sag. TONE does nothing here; use it with BOOST as a clean EQ preamp.
VIBE
The 60s pedal preamp: a low-end lift (below about 250 Hz, growing with DRIVE), soft, uneven (even-harmonic) saturation and a top end that rolls off from about 18 kHz down to 7 kHz as DRIVE rises. TONE does nothing in this voicing - the drive sets the top end.
ZEN
Gentle, symmetrical saturation with a smooth, amp-like breakup.
TRANS
Single-transistor drive: lopsided, it starts biting earlier and gets furry when pushed.
BOOST
Switches the EQ boost in or out. It sits in front of the drive, so a boost also drives the voicing harder.
30/300/3K, 50/500/5K, 70/700/7K
The three frequencies the boost lifts at once, each a decade apart: low, mid and high together.
+1 dB, +3 dB, +6 dB
How much each of the three bands is lifted.

VU meter

Shows the input level after TRIM and GUARD, with 0 VU at -18 dBFS. Under it, the panel names the voicing and how hard it is working: WARM, PUSHED or SATURATED, by the DRIVE setting (CLEAN reads NO DRIVE).

DRIVE

DRIVE
How hard the voicing is driven, 0 to 100% (40% by default). It also deepens the sag compression in front of it.
TONE
A top-end roll-off for ZEN and TRANS, sweeping its corner from about 1.2 kHz (dark) to 14 kHz (open), 0 to 100%.
OUT lights
Six lights showing the output peak level: -36, -24, -12, -6, 0 and +3 dB.

Drift

DRIFT
0 to 100%. 50% is the standard amount; 100% is twice that, 0 turns it off. It makes the saturation's lopsidedness (bias) wander slowly and the sag compression breathe in and out. Higher settings also make the wander move faster.

IN / OUT

TRIM
Input level before anything else, from -24 to +12 dB.
GUARD
Click the TRIM knob (without turning it) to switch GUARD on or off; the small light at the knob's upper right shows it is on and glows brighter while it works. On by default. Gently eases very hot sources down towards about -18 dBFS average and catches fast peaks above -6 dBFS, so loud sources never overdrive the plugin. It never boosts quiet sources.
SAT
Band saturation on the way out: only the lows and highs are saturated (the mids stay clean), with a soft curve that loosens as the level rises.
OFF / CLIP / LIMIT
The final stage: nothing, a soft clipper that holds the peaks just under 0 dBFS (no latency), or a lookahead limiter (3 ms latency, compensated by REAPER). The CLIP key flashes red while the clipper is working.

MASTER

MIX
Dry / preamp balance, 0 to 100% (100% by default). Lower settings blend the clean signal back in for parallel drive.
OUTPUT
Output level, -24 to +12 dB.

The header

PRESETS, ◄ ► and the display
PRESETS (or a click on the display) opens the list of factory presets; ◄ and ► step through them. A preset sets the voicing, DRIVE, TONE, the EQ boost, the drift amounts and MIX; the drift speed, OUTPUT and the input and output stages are left as they are. The last entry resets everything to the defaults.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual. Click anywhere to close it.

Tips

  • Use the VU: aim for the needle around 0 with TRIM before you set DRIVE, so the voicings react as designed.
  • BOOST adds level as well as colour, so it drives the voicing harder too; pull DRIVE back a little when you switch it on.
  • For parallel drive on drums or bass, turn DRIVE up and MIX down to 30 - 50%.
  • TONE only works in ZEN and TRANS; in VIBE the drive itself darkens the top.
