Drum machines

G Clockwork

An analogue preset-rhythm box: 15 synthesised voices, 32 built-in rhythms, ten kits and a phaser.

Overview

G Clockwork is a rhythm box in the spirit of the late-70s preset-rhythm machines. All 15 voices are synthesised, with no samples: kick, snare, rimshot, closed and open hat, cymbal, high and low bongo, conga, clave, maracas, guiro, tambourine, cowbell and metal beat. Each voice has its own TUNE, DECAY and LEVEL, and the KITS keys re-voice the whole machine in one go.

You can play it two ways, or both at once. From MIDI, each voice answers to its General MIDI drum note. Or switch on PLAY and it runs one of 32 built-in rhythms - 16 full-kit rhythms and 16 hand-percussion rhythms - locked to REAPER's tempo while the transport runs, with a B variation, automatic fills, accent and swing. A built-in phaser, the GFX instrument drift and per-hit variation keep it sounding like a real, slightly unruly machine.

Reach for it for

  • Vintage drum-machine grooves for demos, songwriting and lo-fi tracks
  • Hand-percussion loops to play along with (the PERC rhythms)
  • Programming analogue-style beats from MIDI
  • Swirling, phased drum loops
  • Adding a shaker, tambourine or cowbell part under a live kit

Getting started

  1. Insert a new track and add G Clockwork to it as an instrument. It makes its own sound and needs no audio input.
  2. To play the built-in rhythms: click the display (or RHYTHMS) and pick a rhythm, switch PLAY on, then start REAPER's transport. The rhythm follows the project tempo and stops when the transport stops.
  3. To play it from MIDI: record-arm the track with a MIDI input, or draw notes in a MIDI item on the track. Each voice answers to its General MIDI drum note (shown in the NOTE column). The MIDI light in the header flashes on each note.
  4. Both work at the same time: leave PLAY on and add extra hits from MIDI on top.

Controls

The header

Display
Shows the rhythm's number and name, whether it is a KIT RHYTHM or PERCUSSION, VAR A or VAR B, and its feel (4/4, 3/4 or TRIPLET). On the right: the current BAR (and FILL while a fill plays), WAIT while PLAY is on and the transport is stopped, or MIDI ONLY when PLAY is off. Click it to open the rhythm list.
< / >
Step to the previous or next rhythm.
RHYTHMS
Opens the rhythm list, in two groups: KIT RHYTHMS and PERCUSSION ONLY.
MIDI
Lights briefly on each incoming MIDI note.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Next to the S / M / L size keys. Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual. Click anywhere to close it.

The voices

The 15 voices sit in two columns. Click a voice's name to hear it. Double-click a knob to reset it.

VOICE
The voice's name tab. Click it to hear the voice.
NOTE
The MIDI note that plays the voice: KICK 36 (and 35), SNARE 38 (and 40), RIMSHOT 37, CLOSED HAT 42 (and 44), OPEN HAT 46, CYMBAL 49 (and 51, 52, 55, 57, 59), BONGO HIGH 60, BONGO LOW 61, CONGA 62 (and 63, 64), CLAVE 75, MARACAS 70, GUIRO 73 (and 74), TAMBOURINE 54, COWBELL 56, METAL BEAT 80 (and 81). Note velocity sets how hard each hit is.
TUNE
The voice's pitch, -12 to +12 semitones.
DECAY
How long the voice rings, 25 to 400% of its normal length.
LEVEL
The voice's level in the mix, 0 to 100%.
HIT
Lights in the voice's colour each time it plays.

The closed hat cuts off the open hat, as on the original machines. Each voice also has its own fixed place in the stereo picture, which SPREAD widens or narrows.

RHYTHM

PLAY
Plays the selected rhythm while REAPER's transport runs, locked to the project's tempo and bars. Off: the machine only plays from MIDI.
VAR B
Adds the variation parts on top of the basic rhythm (extra percussion, open hats, cymbals).
PERC
Switches between a kit rhythm and its percussion-only partner (rhythm 1 and 17, 2 and 18, and so on).
FILL
Drops a fill into the second half of the last bar: OFF, EVERY 2, EVERY 4 or EVERY 8 bars.
ACCENT
How much the accented hits stand out, 0 to 100% (50% by default). At 100% the unaccented hits play at about half strength.
SWING
Delays the off-beat 16ths, 0 to 100%, for a shuffled feel. Works on the 16-step rhythms only; the triplet and 3/4 rhythms are left as they are.

KITS

CLASSIC ... BRUSHES
Ten keys that re-voice the whole kit (tune, decay and the balance between voices) - see Kits below. The lit key shows the last kit chosen. Your rhythm settings are left alone.
RESET ALL
Every control back to its starting value.

MASTER

SPREAD
How wide the voices are spread across the stereo picture, 0 to 100% (60% by default). 0 puts everything in the centre.
OUTPUT
Output level, -24 to +12 dB.
CHANNEL
The MIDI channel the voices listen to: OMNI (all channels, the default) or channel 1 to 16. MIDI is always passed on down the chain.
Meter
Output level, left and right.

PHASER

A six-stage phaser on the whole machine, swept by its own LFO. The display shows the phaser's response as it sweeps. It fades in and out smoothly, so switching it on or off never clicks.

ON
Switches the phaser in or out.
RATE
The sweep speed, 0.05 to 8 Hz (0.3 Hz by default).
DEPTH
How far the sweep travels, 0 to 100% (70% by default).
FEEDBACK
Resonance around the phaser, 0 to 95% (50% by default). Higher settings give a sharper, more whistling sweep.
MIX
How much phaser, 0 to 100% (100% by default).
STEREO
Offsets the right side's sweep from the left, 0 to 180 degrees (90 by default). 0 is a mono phaser; higher settings give wide stereo movement.

DRIFT + FEEL

DRIFT
The GFX instrument drift, 0 to 100% (25% by default): the tuning of the whole kit wanders slowly (up to about 20 cents), the brightness of the noise voices (snare, hats, maracas, tambourine) and the tone of the metal voices (cymbal, cowbell, metal beat) drift, decays vary slightly, and each hit is tuned a little differently. 0 switches it off.
HITS
Makes every hit a little different in tune, decay and level, 0 to 100% (30% by default).
TIMING
Loosens the groove: each hit is delayed by a random amount up to this time, 0 to 20 ms (0 by default). Works on rhythm and MIDI hits alike.

IN / OUT strip

SAT
Band saturation on the way out: only the lows and highs are saturated (the mids stay clean), with a soft curve that loosens as the level rises.
OFF / CLIP / LIMIT (OUT)
The final stage: nothing, a soft clipper (no latency), or a lookahead limiter that holds the peaks at -0.3 dBFS (3 ms latency, compensated by REAPER). CLIP is on by default; its key flashes red while it is clipping.
MONO
Makes the low end mono below this frequency, from OFF up to 500 Hz.
WIDTH
Widens the stereo picture, from +0% (the default) to +100%.

Presets

Kits

Each kit first sets every voice back to its normal level, then changes the tune, decay and balance.

CLASSIC
The machine as designed: every voice at its normal tune and decay.
TIGHT
Tight & Dry: every voice shortened to 55% (the kick to 70%).
DEEP
Deep & Boomy: tuned down 3 semitones with longer decays; the kick 4 semitones down with twice its decay.
BRIGHT
Tuned up 3 semitones (the kick 2), slightly shorter.
LONG
Long Tails: every voice rings much longer (220%, the kick 160%).
DISCO
A little higher and tighter, with a deep, long kick and louder kick, hats and tambourine.
LATIN
Latin Percussion: kick and snare down, bongos, conga, clave, maracas, guiro and cowbell up.
LO-FI
Lo-Fi Box: tuned down and shorter, with quieter hats and cymbal.
ELECTRO
Short and slightly low, with a very deep, long kick, a punchy short snare, and louder kick and snare.
BRUSHES
Jazz Brushes: a long, low, soft snare, a quieter kit, longer decays and a shorter kick.

Tips

  • Pick a kit rhythm, then press PERC to hear its percussion partner - great for adding a shaker or conga part under a live drummer.
  • Turn FILL to EVERY 4 or EVERY 8 so long loops don't sound stuck.
  • A little SWING (20-40%) and a few ms of TIMING make the 16-step rhythms feel much less mechanical.
  • For a classic swirling drum-machine sound, switch the PHASER on with a slow RATE and FEEDBACK around 50-70%.
  • Use the MIDI notes to add your own hits on top of a running rhythm - a crash on the one, an extra conga, a cowbell part.
