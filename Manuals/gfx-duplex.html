Dynamics

Duplex

A two-channel compressor, de-esser and peak limiter for stereo, dual-mono or mid-side work.

Overview

Duplex is a dual-channel dynamics processor in the spirit of the classic 80s British two-channel units. Each channel is a clean VCA compressor with a gentle 6 dB soft knee, a frequency-conscious de-esser and a separate fast peak limiter.

The two channels can work three ways. In STEREO, channel A runs both sides together. In DUAL, A handles the left side and B the right. In M-S, A handles the mid (the centre of the image) and B the side (the edges), so you can compress the middle and the width separately.

The detector measures the average level over a few milliseconds rather than single peaks. AUTO timing follows the music: the attack speeds up the further the signal goes over, and the release lets go quickly after short peaks and slowly after long passages of compression.

Reach for it for

  • Vocals: compression and de-essing in one place
  • Gentle glue on a stereo bus
  • Taming sibilance with SPLIT de-essing
  • Compressing the centre and the sides of a mix separately (M-S)
  • Dual-mono work on two unrelated sources
  • Parallel drum compression

Controls

CHANNEL A and CHANNEL B

The two channels have the same controls. The label beside each name shows its job in the current MODE: BOTH SIDES, LEFT or RIGHT, MID or SIDE. In STEREO mode channel B rests and its panel says so.

IN
Switches the channel in or out. With it out, the audio passes straight through that channel.
THRESH
The level where compression starts, -40 to 0 dB (-20 dB by default).
RATIO
How strongly the channel compresses, 1:1 to INF:1 (3:1 by default). Fully right is INF:1 - limiting.
ATTACK
0.1 to 50 ms (5 ms by default). Reads AUTO when AUTO is on.
RELEASE
20 ms to 2 s (200 ms by default). Reads AUTO when AUTO is on.
GAIN
Make-up gain, -10 to +20 dB.
DE-ESS (knob)
The de-ess frequency, 2 to 12 kHz (6 kHz by default).
LIMIT
A separate fast peak limiter after the compressor, -24 to 0 dB: instant attack, 50 ms release. Fully right (0 dB) is OFF. The red light beside it shows it working.
AUTO
Program-dependent timing, replacing ATTACK and RELEASE: the further over the threshold, the faster the attack; the release is quick after short peaks and slows down after long passages of compression.
DE-ESS (button)
The compressor listens only above the DE-ESS frequency, so it reacts to sibilance rather than to the whole voice.
SPLIT
On by default; used with DE-ESS. The gain reduction is applied only above the de-ess band (the split sits at three-quarters of the DE-ESS frequency, so the whole ess band is caught): the esses come down and the body of the voice is untouched. Off: the whole signal is turned down, as on the original units.
GR
The row of 12 lights under each channel shows its gain reduction (compressor and limiter together), from 1 to 30 dB.

MASTER

MODE
STEREO: channel A runs both sides, with one shared detector. DUAL: A = left, B = right. M-S: A = mid, B = side.
LINK
Always lit in STEREO. In DUAL and M-S it is a switch (on by default): the two detectors share the louder level, so the image stays put, and the two channels' controls are ganged - turning one moves the other by the same amount, and any offset you set while unlinked is kept.
MIX
Parallel compression, 0 to 100% (shown as WET): blends the dry signal back in. 100% is fully processed.
IN / OUT meters
Input and output level. Click them to reset the peak hold.

The header

PRESETS
Opens the list of factory presets (or click the preset display). The ◄ / ► keys step through them. Choosing one sets both channels and the MASTER section. The last entry resets everything to the defaults.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual. Click anywhere to close it.

Duplex has no input or output fold-out. An input guard is on by default, gently easing very hot sources down towards about -18 dBFS average and catching fast peaks above -6 dBFS; it can be switched off from REAPER's parameter list (Input guard).

Tips

  • For de-essing, switch DE-ESS on, sweep the DE-ESS knob until the GR lights react mostly to the esses, then set THRESH so they come down by a few dB.
  • Leave SPLIT on for vocals: only the top end is turned down, so the voice doesn't dip on every 's'.
  • In M-S, a little extra GAIN on channel B (side) widens the mix; compressing B harder than A holds the edges in.
  • With LINK on in DUAL or M-S, set one channel and the other follows; switch LINK off to give the two channels different settings, then switch it back on to move them together.
