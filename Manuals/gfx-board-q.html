EQ & filters

Board EQ

A four-band channel EQ in the spirit of the classic British large-format console strips.

Overview

Board EQ is a console channel EQ: a high-pass and low-pass filter, a low and high band that can be shelves or bells, and two fully parametric mid bands. It ends in the channel's line amp, which can be driven into gentle saturation.

Two voicings recreate the two well-known generations of console EQ card. BROWN is the earlier, gentler card, with broad bells and soft shelves. BLACK is the later card: the bells tighten as you boost or cut further, and the shelves have a little dip or bump just inside the corner, for a more focused, punchy sound.

The display on the right shows the response of the whole EQ, with a coloured dot for each band.

Reach for it for

  • Everyday channel EQ on vocals, drums, bass and guitars
  • Punchy, focused boosts with the BLACK voicing
  • Broad, musical tone shaping with the BROWN voicing
  • Cleaning up the ends with the HPF and LPF
  • A touch of line-amp warmth (DRIVE)

Controls

FILTERS

HPF
A high-pass filter, 18 dB per octave, from OFF (fully left) up to 400 Hz. Removes rumble and low-end mud.
LPF
A low-pass filter, 12 dB per octave, from 3 kHz up to OFF (fully right). Tames hiss and harsh top.

LF

GAIN
-15 to +15 dB.
FREQ
30 to 450 Hz (100 Hz by default).
BELL
Off (default): a low shelf. On: a bell centred on FREQ.

LMF

GAIN
-15 to +15 dB.
FREQ
200 Hz to 2.5 kHz (600 Hz by default).
Q
The bandwidth, 0.4 to 3 (1 by default). Higher is narrower. In BLACK the bell also narrows as you boost or cut more.

HMF

GAIN
-15 to +15 dB.
FREQ
600 Hz to 7 kHz (2.5 kHz by default).
Q
The bandwidth, 0.4 to 3 (1 by default).

HF

GAIN
-15 to +15 dB.
FREQ
1.5 to 16 kHz (8 kHz by default).
BELL
Off (default): a high shelf. On: a bell centred on FREQ.

LINE AMP

DRIVE
Pushes the channel's line amp into gentle saturation, 0 to 100%. It is level matched, so it adds colour rather than volume.
OUTPUT
Output level, -12 to +12 dB.
EQ IN
Switches the four EQ bands in or out (in by default). The HPF and LPF stay in.
BROWN / BLACK
The voicing (BLACK by default). BROWN: broad bells and soft shelves. BLACK: bells that tighten as you boost or cut (proportional Q), and shelves with a small opposite dip just inside the corner.

Display

Response curve
The frequency response of the filters, the EQ and OUTPUT together, from 20 Hz to 20 kHz and -18 to +18 dB. While the EQ is in, a dot in each band's cap colour shows its frequency and gain.

The header

PRESETS
Opens the list of factory presets; the arrows step through them and the display shows the one in use (click it for the list too). A preset sets all the EQ and line-amp controls (and puts the EQ in, OUTPUT at 0 dB). The last entry resets everything to the defaults.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual. Click anywhere to close it.

Board EQ has no input or output stage on its panel: there is no TRIM, SAT or OUT stage, as the line amp does that job. An input guard runs in the background (on by default), gently easing very hot sources down so they never overdrive the line amp.

Tips

  • Try the same settings in BROWN and BLACK: BROWN for gentle, broad shaping, BLACK when a boost needs to be focused and punchy.
  • In BLACK, big cuts get narrow on their own, so you can cut a resonance hard without hollowing out the sound around it.
  • Use EQ IN to compare with and without the bands; the filters stay in, so the comparison is fair.
  • A little DRIVE (10 - 25%) adds density without obvious distortion.
